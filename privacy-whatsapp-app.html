Privacy Policy — WhatsApp Business App

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Short Term Storage L.L.C. (trading as "PurpleBox Storage") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with us through our WhatsApp Business application powered by the Meta (Facebook) WhatsApp Business API.

By sending us a message on WhatsApp or receiving messages from us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. About Our App

We use the WhatsApp Business API (via Meta/Facebook Developer Platform) to:

  • Respond to customer inquiries about our self-storage, packing, and moving services
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and service updates
  • Provide customer support and answer questions
  • Send promotional messages (only with your prior opt-in consent)

3. Information We Collect

When you communicate with us via WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • WhatsApp phone number: Your phone number associated with your WhatsApp account
  • WhatsApp profile name: The display name you have set on WhatsApp
  • Message content: Text messages, images, documents, or voice messages you send to us
  • Timestamps: Date and time of messages sent and received
  • Message status: Delivery and read receipts

We do not access your WhatsApp contacts, call logs, location data, or any other information from your device beyond what you explicitly share in your messages.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through WhatsApp to:

  • Respond to your inquiries about storage units, pricing, and availability
  • Process and confirm bookings and reservations
  • Send service-related notifications (e.g., move-in reminders, payment confirmations)
  • Provide customer support and resolve issues
  • Send promotional offers (only if you have opted in)
  • Improve our services and customer experience

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Consent: You initiate contact with us on WhatsApp or opt in to receive messages
  • Contractual necessity: To fulfill services you have requested (e.g., booking confirmations)
  • Legitimate interest: To provide customer support and improve our services
  • Legal obligation: To comply with applicable UAE laws and regulations

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data may be shared with:

  • Meta (Facebook): As the provider of the WhatsApp Business API, Meta processes message delivery. Their use of data is governed by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • Our service providers: CRM and helpdesk platforms that help us manage customer communications (under strict data processing agreements)
  • Legal authorities: When required by UAE law, court orders, or government regulations

7. Data Storage & Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect your information:

  • Messages are transmitted using WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption during transit
  • Our systems use secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access to customer data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We conduct regular security reviews of our systems and processes

8. Data Retention

We retain your WhatsApp communication data for:

  • Active customers: For the duration of your service agreement plus 2 years
  • Inquiries (non-customers): Up to 12 months from your last interaction
  • Legal requirements: As long as required by applicable UAE law

After these periods, your data is securely deleted or anonymized.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out: Stop receiving messages by replying "STOP" or contacting us
  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw your consent for us to contact you at any time

To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp or email us at the contact details below.

10. Third-Party Links

Messages we send may contain links to our website or third-party services (e.g., payment portals, Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. Please review their privacy policies separately.

11. Children's Privacy

Our WhatsApp Business service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through WhatsApp.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. If we make significant changes, we will notify you via WhatsApp where appropriate.

13. Data Deletion Request

If you wish to have all your data deleted from our systems, you can:

  • Send us a WhatsApp message requesting data deletion
  • Email us at the address below with "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion, unless we are legally required to retain certain data.
