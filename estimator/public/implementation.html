1 · Product strategy

Keep the estimator focused on choosing belongings, choosing a unit, understanding price and submitting a useful request. Ask for contact information only at the end. Keep 3D optional: it builds confidence but should not block price discovery. Home bundles are editable starting inventories, not a bedroom-count calculator.

Challenge: do not add a six-option welcome menu inside the estimator; that belongs in WhatsApp. Do not request a budget before displaying transparent pricing. Do not ask customers to understand square feet. Do not label future availability as guaranteed based on today’s stock. Do not turn an online request into a reservation without an inventory hold.

3 · Storage estimation methodology

Use the supplied three floor plans, preserving eligibility exclusions and irregular outlines. Convert each item to its packed bounding dimensions. Beds become upright mattress/frame bundles; tables become upright tops with bundled legs. Customers must confirm dismantling and bundle thickness. Mattresses use upright catalogue dimensions. Pack individual objects into usable chair/sofa seat areas, shelf interiors and furniture tops before creating floor stacks. Try alternate box orientations where contents permit. Suitcases default to lying flat. Customers can disable unsuitable surfaces, mark heavy/fragile contents and prohibit box turning. Chair nesting remains a separate confirmed option. Check full support, furniture collision bodies, shelf clearances and actual unit height. Surface strength and weight remain customer assumptions.

Try three packing orders and both 90° floor orientations. Check boundaries, overlaps, an optional 60 cm front access strip and the 2.4 m height (1 m for 10 sq ft lockers). Prefer the smallest fitting option with at least 10% spare area relative to packed footprints where found. This is an area allowance, not a guarantee of handling space or doorway access. Show a tighter alternative only if every item was placed. Manual layouts validate every movement; furniture and its contents move as a group.

Round tables render as round but reserve a conservative rectangular bounding footprint. Chair nesting requires customer confirmation. No sofa tipping, storage underneath tables, load-bearing calculation or door-path simulation is claimed. Those need item-specific verified clearances and safe packing rules; silently applying a blanket efficiency factor could underestimate space.

Download the detailed item database. Includes 101 household/business items, dimensions in metres, model type, shape, category, vertical/dismantling capability, carton stack limits, fragility hints and source notes. Estimated dimensions remain customer-editable. Fragility hints are preliminary rather than a complete handling classification. Categories filter individual items and never add a preset list. 5 · Pricing engine

Requirements above the largest configured single unit round up to the next 50 sq ft level and combine size bands, preferring fewer units and larger components. Both 240 and 250 sq ft therefore offer 200 + 50 sq ft with the default bands. Combined offers require staff confirmation of the split layout; one reference-plan 3D model is not a packing proof across separate units. Counts are checked for each component. A selected padlock is included per unit.

Deterministic, server-side pricing with revision checking: choose the commercial size band at or above the required plan area, with no downward tolerance; disclose both plan area and billing band. This mapping must be replaced with operational unit-to-product IDs before launch. Dates use Dubai time. Active promotions require enabled status and valid start/end dates.

Storage = (configured 4-week rate / 4) × selected weeks − applicable discounted weeks. Choose any integer stay from 2 to 52 weeks. Subtotal = storage + selected padlock + configured moving/packing. VAT = subtotal × configured percentage, rounded to cents. Total = subtotal + VAT. Quote shows storage, fees, VAT and total separately. A duration is required before quotation. Unpriced services are explicitly excluded and referred to staff.

Rates start in draft mode. No historical promotion is automatically activated. Negotiated rates and long-term exceptions require staff approval; do not let a customer supply a rate. Submitted quotes are recalculated on the server and saved as snapshots; a changed configuration revision asks the customer to refresh.

7 · Human handoff and WhatsApp mechanism

Contact promptly for a storage/visit request, move-in within 7 days, moving, business storage, over 200 sq ft or a manual quotation. Staff see the reason, inventory, area, date, duration, services, pricing snapshot, contact details, source and consent. No autonomous negotiation or confirmation of a reservation.

Now: staff open a prepared WhatsApp reply from the request. Customers can open the configured business number after submission with their request reference. No automated sending is enabled.

Next: connect the existing WhatsApp Business Platform account to a server-side integration worker. Receive inbound-message and delivery-status webhooks; verify signatures; map the customer to a CRM lead; issue a short-lived opaque estimator entry token. Resolve known contact details server-side rather than putting phone numbers in URLs. Store request updates in the ERP using idempotent request IDs and a retry/outbox queue.

Use deterministic routes for pricing, inventory and follow-up timing. Optional AI may classify unusual WhatsApp questions; unknown or low-confidence answers go to a person. The 3D experience stays in the web application; do not duplicate it in WhatsApp Flows. Use WhatsApp selectable messages for FAQ, visit and human-assistance routes.

Proposed follow-up: urgent → staff review during staffed hours; within 30 days → one useful check-in near the move date; longer-term → consented reminder around 30 days before the expected date; researching → a low-frequency useful message only with opt-in. Suppress after reply, booking, opt-out or loss status. Check inventory again before making any availability statement.

For API delivery, use free-form service replies only within the customer-service window; use approved templates outside it, with appropriate consent. Sources: Meta service messages, Meta template fundamentals, Meta opt-in guidance. Reviewed 13 September 2026.

8 · Sales workspace

Request queue with score and reasons, searchable name/source/size, new/hot/business/moving/visit/nurture filters, full inventory, status, owner assignment and follow-up date. WhatsApp reply and copy-summary actions preserve context. The first version shows the latest 500 requests; pagination, audit history and granular staff roles are production extensions.

9 · Admin workspace

Protected by server-side staff allowlist. Staff can edit price bands, counts, promotions, VAT, padlock, optional service charges, facility location/access, media URLs, WhatsApp number, follow-up drafts and scoring weights. Revision checks prevent silently overwriting a concurrent saved change. Unknown/stale counts remain unconfirmed. Facility-specific unit IDs, multiple locations, negotiated price approvals, FAQ authoring and editable geometric floor plans are future extensions, not hidden customer controls.

10 · Data model

Current database tables: settings (revisioned configuration); leads (immutable submitted snapshot plus status, score, assignment and follow-up); events (session, stage, campaign and timestamp). Item catalogue and plan geometry remain versioned source data, with downloadable item JSON.

Production normalized model: Locations → Units → Availability/Holds; Products → Rates → Promotions; Leads → Inventories → InventoryItems; Leads → Quotes → QuoteLines; Leads → Requests → Reservations → Payments; Leads → ConsentRecords and MessageEvents. External CRM IDs join lead records; a transactional outbox owns sync retries and an audit log records staff changes. Store contact details only on protected records.

11 · Architecture

Mobile web interface + local Three.js viewer → same-origin Worker API → D1 database. All prices, fit rechecks and lead scores are computed server-side for submitted requests. Staff routes require verified platform identity and an explicit allowlist; customer routes are anonymous-compatible when the site is eventually made public. Current review access remains private.

ERP integration should own operational inventory and rate approvals. Add atomic date-based holds with expiration before online booking. Payment-provider success webhooks, not browser redirects, confirm paid reservations. Keep credentials server-side. Optional AI belongs behind separate classification/photo-review endpoints, never in arithmetic, availability or booking authority.

12 · Mobile screen design

Six compact screens: property bundles, room starters and catalogue; side-by-side item/dimension list; recommended unit with optional 3D; duration/timing cards; line-item quotation with availability; request action and two contact fields. A fixed next button provides one-handed navigation. Contact details are not requested until the customer has seen the quote. The working estimator is the interactive wireframe.

Language extension: keep catalogue IDs, status codes and API values language-independent; extract visible labels to locale dictionaries and add document lang/dir plus Arabic typography and RTL layout tests. Arabic translation is not included in this release.
