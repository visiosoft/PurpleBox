Questions

Before you book

How is storage priced?
Every 4 weeks, based on unit size. No setup or admin fees. VAT at the standard UAE rate. Cancel any time with 14 days’ notice, no penalty.
Are units climate-controlled?
All units have AC and humidity control, which protects furniture, electronics and documents from Dubai summers.
What’s the minimum period?
1-week minimum stay. Rent is billed every 4 weeks, and you can extend for as long as you need or lock in a longer period for better value.
What size storage unit do I need?
As a rough guide: 10-25 sq ft for a studio or 1BHK, 35-50 sq ft for a 1-2 bedroom apartment, 75-100 sq ft for a 2-3 bedroom home, 150-200 sq ft for a full villa. Not sure? Choose “Not sure yet” in the form and we will help.
