We match your item dimensions against sizes in the three Al Quoz floor plans. This is an estimate, not confirmation of availability or a guaranteed fit.

Doors and columns need confirmation. Units are 2.4 m high, except 10 sq ft lockers which are 1 m high. Units with missing or conflicting information are excluded.

What cannot go into storage?

No perishable food, plants or animals; explosives, fireworks or ammunition; flammable fuels, paints or solvents; propane tanks, LPG bottles or other gas cylinders; corrosive, toxic or hazardous chemicals; illegal or stolen goods; waste, wet or pest-infested items.

No propane tank — even with a barbecue. Appliances must be emptied, clean and dry. Ask PurpleBox before bringing batteries, liquids or anything you are unsure about.
