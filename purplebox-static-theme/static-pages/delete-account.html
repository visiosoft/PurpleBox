Delete your PurpleBox Storage account

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This page explains how customers of the PurpleBox Storage app (by PurpleBox / Short Term Storage LLC, Dubai) can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

Send a request from the phone number or email address registered on your PurpleBox account:

  • Email: info@purplebox.ae with the subject "Account deletion request", or
  • WhatsApp our team using the chat in the app or at purplebox.ae.

Include your full name and registered phone number.

We will verify your identity and confirm deletion within 30 days.

If you have an active storage contract or unpaid balance, we will first help you close the contract and collect your belongings. The account can be deleted once the contract has ended.

What we delete

  • Your app account and login (phone number)
  • Profile details: name, email address, phone number and addresses
  • Photos and documents you uploaded in the app
  • Saved bookings, pickup requests and app preferences

What we keep, and for how long

  • Invoices, payment records and signed storage contracts are kept for the period required by UAE commercial and tax law (generally up to 5 years), then deleted.
  • Card details are never stored by PurpleBox; payments are processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

Questions?

Contact us at info@purplebox.ae or call Customer Service on +971 54 404 6407. See also our Privacy Policy.
