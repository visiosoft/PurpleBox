How clean are the storage units?

Every unit is cleaned before each move-in, the facility is kept clean throughout, and we run regular professional pest control. Get a free quote →

Are the units air-conditioned?

Yes. The whole storage facility is climate controlled to protect your belongings from Dubai heat and humidity. Get a free quote →

How secure is my storage unit?

Our facility has SIRA-regulated 24/7 CCTV, face-recognition entry for approved customers and 24/7 fire monitoring. Each unit is individually locked. Get a free quote →

Can I access my unit at night?

Yes. You have 24/7 access to your storage unit. Get a free quote →

What is the minimum rental period?

One 4-week billing cycle. After that you can continue cycle by cycle for as long as you need. Get a free quote →

What can't I store?

No perishable food, plants or animals; explosives, fireworks or ammunition; flammable fuels, paints or solvents; propane tanks, LPG bottles or other gas cylinders; corrosive, toxic or hazardous chemicals; illegal or stolen goods; or waste, wet or pest-infested items.
